The People

Family Run. Community Loved.

Little Bones is Mat Taylor's labour of love — a brand new structure built from the ground up with his dad's help, who donated the electrical work and more. At the grill every single day alongside Mat is his mom, who has been part of the operation for three years running.

"Mom knows everybody," Mat laughs. It's routine to have customers who know each other, and know the family. That's exactly the kind of place Little Bones is — not just somewhere you eat, but somewhere you belong.

Our Story

Born & Raised in Norwich

Little Bones Grill started with a vision: bring real, craft-made food to the small town of Norwich, Ontario — the kind of food that punches way above its weight. Mat Taylor, born and raised right here, couldn't imagine opening up anywhere else.

What started as a humble chip wagon quickly became something far more interesting. Mat transformed it into a proper sandwich shack with a creekside patio, serving up inventive, ingredient-driven sandwiches, smoked meats, hot dogs, and rotating weekly specials.

Every item on the menu has a story — most named after songs by The Tragically Hip. The Henhouse, the El Dorado, the Sled Dogs. Good music. Good food. Good people.

"At this point, there's no possible way I could do this without her." — Mat Taylor, on his mom

The Setting

Small Town. Real Food.

Norwich is the kind of place where the grill owner's mom already knows your name — and so does the person behind you in line. Tucked just off the main road at 7 Stover Street, Little Bones has quietly become part of the town's rhythm.

The shack itself was built from scratch. String lights hang overhead, a creekside patio fills up fast on a good day, and the lineup moves at just the right pace. People show up on a Wednesday like clockwork — regulars, families, and the occasional drive-in from out of town who heard about the Henhouse and had to see for themselves.

It's not just a place to grab lunch. It's a reason to get outside.
