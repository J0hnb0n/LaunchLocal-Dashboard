Headline: 2025 was a breakout growth year. Guests nearly doubled year-over-year (499 vs. 252, +98%), paid ads returned 11.1× blended ROAS, SEO climbed 55 positions, and the 5.0★ reputation held across 29 reviews. The core unlock now is occupancy — at 14.6% on average, the product is working but the room is mostly empty. The margin lives in filling sessions that already exist, not in building new supply.
What worked
Wins to repeat

Paid media printed money

11.1×blended ROAS on $530.90 spend produced $3,988.79 in revenue. Cost per new customer was $4.85 — well below the $55 drop-in price, so every first visit was profitable on session one.

September was the peak system test

30.9%occupancy, 124 guests, 67 sessions, and the highest click-to-book rate of the year (40.9%). This proves the funnel can fill capacity when awareness, intent, and season align.

SEO went from invisible to page-one-adjacent

#66 → #10.7for "Outdoor Spa Ontario" in six months. That's the difference between zero and meaningful organic discovery — and it happened without paid SEO effort.

Reputation is bulletproof

5.0★across 29 Google reviews, zero below 5 stars. Social grew +22.7% on IG and +70% on Facebook. Brand search lift peaked at 106 in September — ads were also priming direct intent.

Where we're leaving money
Opportunities

Occupancy is the ceiling, not demand

14.6%average fill against 217–248 available monthly sessions. Even a lift to 25% roughly doubles annual guests without any new infrastructure.

Shoulder season collapses

Nov: 8.8%occupancy, Dec: 4.8%. Guests fell from 125 in October to 28 in December. Paid spend was also off in both months — the two worst months were the two unspent months.

Newsletter list has stalled

31 subsand flat since September. With 499 guests this year, the capture rate on-site is effectively a rounding error. Every visit should produce an email.

Average booking value is drifting down

$54.95 → $49.77from August to November. Mix is shifting toward discounted 4-pack and 8-pack redemptions — good for retention, but private bookings (starting at $249.95) are under-represented in the revenue mix.

What to do next
Recommendations

Run a winter "sauna season" campaign

Hot sauna + cold plunge is intuitively more compelling in winter, not less. Match Aug/Sept spend levels ($200–300/mo) with creative built around the contrast experience in snow. Target: lift Nov–Feb occupancy from ~7% to 15%.

Gift cards as the December product

December had 28 guests but brand search was still active. Push a 2-week gift-card campaign mid-November. Revenue books now, sessions redeem in Q1 — exactly when occupancy needs it.

Fix newsletter capture at two points

1) Post-booking confirmation opt-in, 2) post-visit review request also offers newsletter. Goal: 300+ subscribers by mid-2026. A 15%-off first-return offer on subscribe pays for itself.

Push private bookings deliberately

A single private booking ($249.95+) equals 5 drop-ins in revenue and fills an otherwise-empty weekday session. Build a private-focused ad creative and a dedicated landing block on the site.

Automate the review flywheel

29 reviews from 499 guests is a 5.8% review rate. An SMS/email request 48 hours post-visit could triple that. Target: 75+ reviews by end of 2026 while holding 4.9★+.

Climb from #10 to top 5

SEO trajectory is strong but will plateau without content. Publish the "Science of Contrast Therapy" journal series (already drafted on the site) and internally link from every service page. Target: top-3 for "outdoor spa Ontario" by Q3.
