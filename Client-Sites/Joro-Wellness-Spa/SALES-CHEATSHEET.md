## THE 4 FEATURES YOU BUILT

### 1. 🤖 AI Chatbot (24/7 Guest Assistant)
**The problem:** Someone visits at 10pm, has a question, gets no answer, leaves.
**What you built:** A floating chat button on the site. Trained on everything Joro — pricing, what to bring, the experience, getting there, health questions, private bookings. Answers instantly, any time of day.
**The pitch line:** *"Your site is currently silent after hours. This makes it talk."*

### 2. 📋 Smart Private Booking Form
**The problem:** Private events (highest-margin product at $249.95+) are being sent to a Gmail address via a mailto link. No form, no automation, no follow-up — inquiries go cold.
**What you built:** A full intake form — name, contact, event type, group size, preferred date, notes — with a polished confirmation state.
**The pitch line:** *"You're leaving your highest-ticket product on a mailto link. We fixed that."*

### 3. 📧 Automated Re-engagement Emails (30/60/90 day)
**The problem:** Guests who loved their session never hear from Joro again.
**What you built:** Three automated emails triggered by time since last visit:
- **30 days:** Warm personal "we miss you" re-engagement
- **60 days:** Package offer (save 15%) — converts one-timers into regulars
- **90 days:** Seasonal hook tied to Norfolk County (trails, beach, summer)
**The pitch line:** *"Every guest who doesn't come back is revenue you've already earned once and then left behind."*

### 4. ✍️ Wellness Journal (SEO Content)
**The problem:** Joro's site doesn't rank for anything beyond its own name. Someone searching "outdoor spa Ontario" or "cold plunge near me" never finds it.
**What you built:** A live blog with two full AI-written articles — *"The Science of Contrast Therapy"* and *"Your First Cold Plunge"* — in a polished editorial layout.
**The pitch line:** *"Right now Google can't find you. This changes that."*
