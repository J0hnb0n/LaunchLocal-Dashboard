Getting to Jörð Spa

Nestled inside Long Point Eco-Adventures in Turkey Point, Ontario — where nature meets renewal.

Finding the Spa

Jörð Spa is located on-site at Long Point Eco-Adventures. The property accommodates many types of guests for the activities available. You may experience a short walk — enjoy the nature as a part of your arrival. You will find the road to Jörð as you follow the road right of the welcome centre.

There is a map at the welcome centre stairs that also provides further directions to the spa.

When You Arrive

Parking & Access

Park in the main lot at Long Point Eco-Adventures. From there, follow the road to the right of the welcome centre — the short walk through the property is part of the experience.

Check the map posted at the welcome centre stairs for a quick visual guide to the spa entrance.
