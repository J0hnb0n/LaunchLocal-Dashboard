FAQ

Common questions before you pour.

How soon can you start?

Most residential driveways and pads go in within 2–4 weeks of sign-off, weather permitting. Larger foundations and ag projects we'll give you a realistic window at the quote stage.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. We come to the site, measure, talk through options and email a written quote — no charge, no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — full liability coverage and WSIB on the crew. We'll send the certificates with your quote if you'd like to see them.

How long until I can drive on a new driveway?

Walk on it after 24–48 hours. Light vehicle use after 7 days. Full strength (heavy trucks, loaded trailers) at 28 days. We'll leave you written cure guidelines.

Should I seal my new concrete?

In Ontario's freeze–thaw climate, yes — we recommend sealing after the slab has cured (usually 30 days in) and then every 2–3 years. We can handle first sealing or point you to a product.

Do you handle removal of old concrete?

Yes. Tear-out, haul-away and disposal are included in most replacement quotes. Grading and sub-base prep too.

Do you work on farms?

A lot of our work is agricultural — barn floors, feed bunks, silage pads, bunker walls, manure tanks. We understand the schedules farms run on and plan around them.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cheque, e-transfer, or bank transfer. Deposit and progress schedule spelled out in the quote — no surprises.
