The premise

What IS the Sock Project?

It's simple. It's fun. It's a small moment of peace in an otherwise literally crazy time.

I'm creating 100(ish) completely unique sock kits — each one dyed specifically for one owner and one owner only.

  • 100(ish) pairs.
  • 100(ish) dedicated fibre artists.
  • One mission: to keep your toesies toasty.

Every person who joins gets a completely unique colourway — one full-sized skein and two coordinating minis — to craft into their favourite pair of socks. You're not just buying yarn. You're becoming part of the project. (And if you want more socks, you can upsize — could even make a sweater.)

What you'll get + how it works

One skein, two minis, dyed deliberately.

Each kit centres on one full-sized skein plus two coordinating mini skeins, dyed together in a single pot so the colours sing as a set. Knit them as a pair of socks with playful contrast cuffs, heels, and toes — or upsize to a Double, Triple, or Quad pack and use the extra yardage for a sweater, shawl, or blanket.

The full skein is on an 80/20 superwash merino-nylon sock base — soft, hard-wearing, and built for feet. The minis are dyed on the same base so they blend seamlessly.

The Sock Project is intentionally simple. There are no major risks, no complicated moving parts, and no external suppliers that could delay things. Every kit is dyed, packaged, and prepared by me in my home studio — so the entire process is fully within my control (aside from shipping). The only thing I want to call out is timing.

Every 25 backers, I dye up the next round of kits. Please share with friends and family — I'm on a mission in 2026 to make this dream come true, and I'd love to share it with creators like you. My goal is to make the process smooth, transparent, and fun for everyone involved. I take a lot of pride in my work, and I'm committed to delivering something beautiful, meaningful, and completely unique to every person who joins.
