Shipping and Orders

How long does shipping take within Canada?

Most orders ship within 2 business days. Canada Post delivery typically takes 3 to 7 business days depending on your province. Free shipping on Canadian orders over $250 CAD.

Do you ship to the United States?

Not at the moment. We ship within Canada only. We hope to open US shipping in the future — sign up for the newsletter to be notified when that happens.

What's your return policy?

Because every skein is hand-dyed and small-batch, we don't accept returns on opened or wound skeins. Unopened skeins in original condition can be returned within 14 days for store credit. Reach out and we'll sort it out together.

Can I pick up my order locally?

Yes — local pickup is available by appointment. Reach out via the contact form and we'll arrange a time.

Yarn

Yarn and Dyeing

What is a dye lot, and why does it matter?

A dye lot is a single batch of yarn dyed at the same time. Hand-dyed yarn varies subtly between batches — even the same colourway dyed two weeks apart can look a little different. For projects that use multiple skeins (sweaters, blankets, big shawls), buy enough from the same lot, and alternate skeins every couple of rows for an even finish. Lot numbers are printed on every label.

What yarn bases do you carry?

Each base is named for one of our studio dogs — from lace-light Bonnie to super-bulky Ellie. Most-stocked are Giddeon (classic 3-ply sock) and Bindi (luxe bulky), with rotating runs of the others. See our yarn base guide above for the full breakdown.

Can I request a custom colourway?

Yes. We take a limited number of custom requests each month. Email us with your inspiration — a photo, paint chip, fabric swatch, or just a description — along with your timeline and the base/weight you'd like. We'll let you know what's possible and quote it for you.

What fibres do you dye?

Our most popular bases are 100% superwash merino (sock weight), an 80/20 merino-nylon (sock weight), and a wool/mohair bulky blend. We rotate other bases — alpaca blends, BFL, silk blends — through the year. Reach out for current availability.

Care

Care and Project Help

How do I care for hand-dyed yarn?

Most of our bases are gentle hand-wash, laid flat to dry. Some sock bases are superwash and can be machine washed on a wool cycle. Always check the label that comes with your skein for specifics. Wash finished pieces with a no-rinse wool wash like Eucalan or Soak.

Will the colour bleed?

We rinse every skein until the water runs clear, but a tiny bit of bleed-off (especially with deeply saturated reds, blues, and purples) is normal on first wash. We recommend washing finished pieces separately the first time.

How much yarn do I need for a project?

It depends on the pattern, your gauge, and the size you're making — your pattern will give a yardage estimate. If you're choosing one of our bases for a specific pattern, send us a note and we'll help match the right base and quantity.
