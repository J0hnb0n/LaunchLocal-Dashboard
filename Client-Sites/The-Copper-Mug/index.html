Our Story

A neighbourhood pub, first and last.

The Copper Mug has held its corner of Broadway Street as Tillsonburg's gathering place — the kind of room where regulars have a stool, the bartender remembers your order, and nobody minds if you linger over a second pint.

Our kitchen keeps it honest: pub classics done properly, shareable starters that taste like they were meant to be shared, and a Sunday roast worth taking your grandmother to. Local drafts on tap, a tidy wine list, and cocktails poured with care.
