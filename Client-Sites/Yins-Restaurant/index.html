Our Story

A neighbourhood kitchen on Main Street.

Yin's Restaurant has been a fixture in downtown Waterford for as long as most folks can remember. Our kitchen turns out the Chinese and Canadian-Chinese classics this town has come to count on — wok-fired, plated generously, and served without fuss.

We're a family-run spot. The menu doesn't chase trends. We make the dishes our regulars have been ordering for years, the same way every time, because that's what keeps a small-town restaurant going for the long haul.

Stop in for lunch, grab dinner on the way home, or call ahead for take-out. We'll have a table or a hot bag ready.
