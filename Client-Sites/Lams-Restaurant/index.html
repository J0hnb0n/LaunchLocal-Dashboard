A Family Kitchen,
Open to Everyone

Lam's Restaurant has been a gathering place for Norwich families for years. What began as a passion for sharing genuine home-cooked Chinese food has grown into one of the most beloved spots in Oxford County — 141 Google reviews and a 4.6-star rating tell part of the story.

Every dish carries the warmth of family tradition — recipes refined across generations, prepared fresh each morning with ingredients chosen for quality and flavour. Nothing on the menu is an afterthought.

When you walk through our door, you're not a customer. You're a guest. We look forward to welcoming you to our table.
