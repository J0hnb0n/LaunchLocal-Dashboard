Our story

A small kitchen, a big family table.

Lily's Tacos began as a love letter from Henry and Lily to the home cooking they grew up with — the markets, the music, the families gathered around late-afternoon tables. Today the dining room and patio at 29 Robinson Street are an extension of their kitchen, and every guest is folded in like family.

“The staff makes us feel like family — and that is the best feeling of all.” — Recent Google review, March 2025

Tortillas are pressed by hand. Salsas are blended in small batches. The carnitas simmer until they fall apart with a fork. Nothing rushed, nothing pre-bagged.
