Our Story

From Norfolk Street
to Three Generations

In 1972, J. Daniels Optical opened its doors on Norfolk Street in Simcoe, becoming a trusted cornerstone of family eye care in Norfolk County.

In the early 1980s, a young optician named Bob Taylor joined the practice. He would later purchase J. Daniels Optical — keeping the name out of deep respect for the legacy and the community's trust it represented.

Now, more than 35 years later, Bob's sons have joined the family business. Dr. Jordan Taylor, optometrist, and Ryan Taylor, office manager, represent the next chapter of a story that began long before they were born.

Welcome to Taylor Optical — Affordable Family Eye Care.
