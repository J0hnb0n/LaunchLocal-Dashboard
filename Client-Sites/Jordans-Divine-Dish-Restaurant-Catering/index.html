Our Story

Good food, plainly said.

Jordan's Divine Dish started the way most good kitchens do — with a recipe box, a patient cook, and a few hungry regulars. We're a family-run restaurant and catering kitchen in the heart of Norwich, feeding neighbours the way our grandmothers fed us: generous plates, warm bread, and nothing fancier than it needs to be.

We bake in-house. We carve meats for the day's specials. We pack catering trays by hand so the lasagna that leaves our kitchen arrives at your event the way it left the oven — bubbling, covered, and ready to serve.

  • Family recipes. Handed down, not googled.
  • Local where we can be. Oxford County produce, Ontario meats.
  • Hospitality that remembers your name. If we've seen you twice, we know your order.
